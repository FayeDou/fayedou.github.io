abstract: >-
  This work surveys deep learning methods for cross-subject EEG decoding, where strong inter-subject variability creates substantial domain shift between training and unseen test subjects. It formalizes the problem as a multi-source domain generalization setting, clarifies subject-independent evaluation protocols, and organizes existing approaches into key methodological families, including feature alignment, adversarial learning, feature disentanglement, and contrastive learning. The survey further discusses theoretical limitations, the role of subject identity, and the emerging potential of EEG foundation models for robust real-world decoding.
