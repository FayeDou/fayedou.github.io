abstract: >-
  This paper presents Glydentify, a deep learning framework for predicting sugar-donor specificity in GT-A and GT-B glycosyltransferases by integrating protein language model embeddings with chemical representations. Trained on large-scale UniProt annotations, Glydentify achieves PR-AUCs of 0.86 and 0.91 for GT-A and GT-B, respectively, outperforming general enzyme–substrate predictors with minimal manual curation. Predictions for uncharacterized plant GTs were tested through in vitro biochemical assays, while residue-level attention analysis suggests that the model captures evolutionary, structural, and biochemical features relevant to donor specificity. These findings support Glydentify’s utility for characterizing GT function and its potential extension to other enzyme families acting on chemically diverse substrates.
