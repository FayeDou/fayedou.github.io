abstract: >-
  This paper presents DeepArrhythmia, a segment-contextualized framework for beat-level ECG arrhythmia classification. Instead of treating each heartbeat as an isolated instance, DeepArrhythmia leverages multi-beat rhythm context, waveform morphology, and explicit physiological measurements to make beat-level predictions. It combines raw ECG signals, rendered waveform images, R-peak localization, numerical rhythm–morphology features, and morphology-focused textual analysis. By selectively routing between minimal and richer evidence based on segment-level confidence, the framework integrates physiological grounding with adaptive evidence acquisition for more context-aware arrhythmia detection.
