abstract: >-
  This paper introduces DynamicDx, a benchmark of 71 neurological consultations across 11 sign categories that evaluates how vision-language models translate patient videos into diagnostic hypotheses and investigations. Across five models, video improves diagnostic accuracy primarily by guiding evidence acquisition, with no reliable benefit from temporal frame order. Providing decisive investigation results raises accuracy to 73.2–93.0%, highlighting evidence acquisition as a key bottleneck. Improved video descriptions and literature retrieval further enhance diagnosis by guiding models toward clinically relevant investigations.
