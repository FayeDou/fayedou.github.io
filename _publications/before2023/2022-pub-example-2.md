abstract: >-
  This paper proposes a deep reinforcement learning-based top-down searching method for indoor localization using Wi-Fi RSSI fingerprints, addressing signal fluctuations caused by environmental uncertainties. The model effectively navigates large indoor spaces, accurately localizing 75% of targets within 0.55 meters in a 25,000 m² area.
