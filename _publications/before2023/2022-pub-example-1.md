abstract: >-
  This article presents a deep reinforcement learning-based approach to 3-D indoor localization using Wi-Fi RSSI fingerprinting, addressing signal variability and environmental dynamics. By formulating localization as a Markov decision process and hierarchically bisecting the search space, the method achieves high accuracy and robustness while reducing time complexity from O(N<sup>3</sup>) to O(log&nbsp;N), outperforming traditional sequential localization methods.
