abstract: >-
  This paper proposes WSF-MAC, a weight-based spatially fair MAC protocol for Underwater Sensor Networks (UWSNs) that addresses space-time uncertainty caused by high propagation delays. By using a silence duration and prioritizing transmissions based on UW-REQ packet timing and competition count, WSF-MAC improves spatial fairness by approximately 10%, as demonstrated in simulation results.
