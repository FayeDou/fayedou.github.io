abstract: >-
  This paper proposes OPMAC, a new medium access control (MAC) protocol designed for time-critical applications in Underwater Wireless Sensor Networks (UWSNs). By establishing an on-demand data transmission pipeline and introducing a lightweight acknowledgment mechanism, OPMAC significantly reduces end-to-end delay and enhances energy efficiency, addressing the challenges of long propagation delays and high error rates in UWSNs.
