abstract: >-
  This paper presents P-MAC, a contention-based MAC protocol designed for mobile Underwater Wireless Sensor Networks (UWSNs), addressing the challenges posed by node mobility and acoustic signal delays. By incorporating an autoregressive AR(5) mobility prediction algorithm to mitigate space-time uncertainty, P-MAC achieves up to 74.8% reduction in delay detection error and improves packet reception rate by 10–15% under wave-induced motion, as demonstrated in NS-2 simulations.
