abstract: >-
  This study evaluates OpenAI's o1-preview model across 14 higher-order cognitive dimensions, finding it outperforms humans in areas like systems thinking, computational thinking, and creative thinking, while slightly underperforming in logical, critical, and quantitative reasoning. Despite its strengths, the model shows limitations in abstract reasoning, underscoring the need for human oversight and suggesting educational systems should adapt to emphasize uniquely human skills in an AI-enhanced world.
