abstract: >-
  This paper proposes a secure underwater localization scheme for wireless sensor networks using cooperative beamforming among AUVs and TDOA-based self-localization, addressing the challenge of privacy leakage in acoustic environments. By formulating a multi-objective optimization problem and solving it with a DRL-based MADDPG algorithm, the method enhances both security and energy efficiency, with validation through simulations and sea experiments.
