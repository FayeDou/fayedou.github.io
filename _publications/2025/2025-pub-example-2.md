abstract: >-
  This paper presents a reinforcement learning–based framework for wireless charging in Wireless Powered Communication Networks (WPCNs). Using a modified Deep Q-learning approach, the method optimizes a Mobile Charger’s trajectory and device charging schedule to balance residual energy and travel distance. Evaluations on both a Gazebo-based simulator and a Turtlebot testbed show superior performance over classical, optimization-based, and other learning-based baselines.
